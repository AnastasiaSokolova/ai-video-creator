Working terms

Plain answers to the usual questions. The detailed scope is confirmed in each written quote.

Ask about your project →
What I need+

Your goal and audience, product or story details, intended platforms, deadline, and visual references. When exact branding matters, your logo and high-resolution product or packaging assets.

Timing+

Estimated after the brief. A short ad often takes 5–7 business days; a more involved film may take longer. The schedule begins once the agreed brief, assets, and booking payment are received.

Payment+

For direct commissions, 50% to book production and the balance before the clean final file is delivered. Marketplace orders follow the platform's payment flow.

Revisions+

Your quote states whether one or two rounds are included. One round means one consolidated list of notes on a delivered version. A new concept, new scenes, or changes to an approved storyboard need a new estimate.

Delivery+

A final MP4 in one agreed aspect ratio. Additional ratios, extra hooks, voiceover, and project or source files are quoted separately. For exact product labels and logos, I composite your original assets rather than relying on AI-generated text.

Usage+

We agree on channels, paid-ad use, music and voice licensing, and any exclusivity in the quote. I can't promise unique ownership of every generated element or guaranteed ad performance.
